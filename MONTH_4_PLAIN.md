# Month 4 — what gets built

Plain version. No code, no file names, no jargon. 20 working days, 10 things a
day. Ticked items are already finished.

Buying things, applying to payment companies, the lawyer and the writer trial
are not here. None of them is work anyone can do at a keyboard, and mixing them
in makes a blocked month look like a failed one.

## After that — the next things worth doing

Not scheduled. These are what the month above makes possible.

**Once writers have used it**
- Find out which advice they acted on and which they ignored
- Drop the advice nobody uses rather than adding more
- Set the pricing against what they actually did, not what they said

**Once short videos have been measured**
- Build up guidance for one niche from real measured videos
- Publish the first finding that no competing tool can state
- Decide whether to buy video data or let writers paste their own

**The bigger pieces, in rough order**
- Two people editing the same script at once
- A better generation process than one request and one answer
- Encrypting script text — easier decided before many writers arrive than after
- A proper mobile experience rather than a page that fits a phone
- Letting a writer carry one story across short, long and screenplay formats

**The ones that are not building**
- A lawyer for the terms and privacy
- Payment company approval
- Deciding the price
- Deciding whether to open sign-ups or stay invitation-only
