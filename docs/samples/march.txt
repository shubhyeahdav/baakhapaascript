FADE IN:

INT. CALL CENTRE, NAXAL - NIGHT

A floor of grey partitions. Headsets. The hum of forty people
saying the same sentence in different voices.

AARATI, 24, mutes her mic and holds a phone under the desk. On
its screen: an acceptance letter. She reads it twice.

SUPERVISOR (O.S.)
Aarati, line four.

She pockets the phone. Unmutes.

AARATI
Good evening, thank you for calling.

INT. CALL CENTRE, BREAK ROOM - NIGHT

KANCHHA, 26, eats dal bhat from a tiffin. Aarati sits. Says
nothing. Pushes her phone across the table.

He reads. Looks up.

KANCHHA
Pokhara?

AARATI
Six weeks.

KANCHHA
Timile aavedan garyeu?

AARATI
In March.

KANCHHA
Ani ghar ma?

She takes the phone back.

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, KITCHEN - MORNING

Small. Clean. A pressure cooker whistles.

BABA, 58, counts hundred-rupee notes into three piles. He wets
his thumb between each one.

AARATI enters, still in last night's clothes.

BABA
Sixty-two thousand. Second instalment.

AARATI
When?

BABA
Friday. They don't take late.

He pushes one pile toward her, taps it twice, then pulls it
back and adds it to the largest pile.

BABA (CONT'D)
Your brother's list came out. Fourth.

AARATI
Fourth.

BABA
In the district.

She looks at the piles.

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, HER ROOM - DAY

A single bed. On the wall, a printed photograph of a road in
Mustang, curling at the corners.

Aarati sits on the floor with a ledger. She writes a number.
Crosses it out. Writes a smaller one.

She takes an envelope from under the mattress. Counts. Twenty-
two thousand.

She puts eighteen back.

EXT. COURTYARD, PATAN - DAY

Aarati and Kanchha walk. He carries a camera bag that is
clearly too heavy for what is in it.

KANCHHA
Rental shop will give me six days. After that they call the
owner.

AARATI
Six is enough.

KANCHHA
Six is enough for the workshop. Not for the fees.

AARATI
I said I'd manage it.

KANCHHA
Ke bhaneko manage?

She stops walking. He stops.

AARATI
I'll do double shifts.

KANCHHA
You're doing double shifts.

INT. CALL CENTRE, NIGHT SHIFT - NIGHT

Aarati at her desk. The clock on the wall: 3:40.

She has two headsets now. One in each ear. She answers one
call, mutes, answers the other.

Her hand shakes when she reaches for water.

INT. CALL CENTRE, SUPERVISOR'S DESK - NIGHT

The SUPERVISOR, 40s, tired rather than unkind, taps a
spreadsheet.

SUPERVISOR
Your average handle time went up.

AARATI
I'll bring it down.

SUPERVISOR
I know you will. I'm telling you before HR does.

She nods. Waits. He goes back to the screen.

SUPERVISOR (CONT'D)
There's a Dashain bonus for anyone doing thirty nights.

AARATI
Thirty.

SUPERVISOR
Consecutive.

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, KITCHEN - MORNING

Baba puts a plate in front of her. She hasn't slept.

BABA
You came at six.

AARATI
Traffic.

BABA
From Naxal.

She eats.

BABA (CONT'D)
Your mother did nights at the hospital. Eleven years. She used
to say the body forgets how to want things.

He sits opposite her with his own plate. Neither eats.

INT. FILM WORKSHOP OFFICE, POKHARA - DAY

Small office. A DIRECTOR, 50s, holds Aarati's phone at arm's
length, on speaker.

DIRECTOR
The seat's yours till the tenth. After that it goes to the
waitlist.

AARATI (V.O.)
Can I pay half?

DIRECTOR
Half holds it a week.

AARATI (V.O.)
And the other half?

DIRECTOR
The other half is the whole thing, nani.

INT. CALL CENTRE, BREAK ROOM - NIGHT

Kanchha slides a bank slip across the table. Aarati doesn't
touch it.

AARATI
Where from?

KANCHHA
Does it matter.

AARATI
Kanchha.

KANCHHA
The camera. I sold it.

She pushes the slip back. He pushes it again.

KANCHHA (CONT'D)
It was a bad camera.

AARATI
It was your camera.

KANCHHA
It was a bad camera and you have four days.

Long beat. She takes it.

EXT. AARATI'S HOUSE - EVENING

Fairy lights over the gate. Plastic chairs in the courtyard.
Someone is testing a speaker.

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, MAIN ROOM - EVENING

Full of relatives. A cake with SANJIV - IOE 4TH iced badly on
it. SANJIV, 19, is being photographed by everyone at once.

Baba is louder than usual. Happy.

BABA
Fourth in the district! Fourth!

AUNT
And Aarati? What is Aarati doing now?

BABA
Aarati is at the call centre. Good company. Foreign clients.

The aunt nods and moves on.

Aarati stands at the edge of the room with a tray she has not
put down.

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Aarati sets the tray down. Sanjiv comes in for more plates.

SANJIV
Didi. Baba said fees are done. Full four years.

AARATI
Full four years?

SANJIV
He said you arranged something.

She looks at him.

SANJIV (CONT'D)
Didi?

INT. AARATI'S HOUSE, MAIN ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Aarati crosses the room. Baba is mid-story. She waits until he
finishes it.

AARATI
You put the whole four years down.

BABA
The discount was for full payment.

AARATI
With what.

BABA
Aarati.

AARATI
With what, Baba.

The room is quieter now than it was. Not silent. Quieter.

BABA
It's a family's money. It doesn't have your name on it or his.

AARATI
Twenty-two thousand of it had my name on it.

BABA
And where were you keeping it? Under a mattress. Like a child.

Sanjiv, in the doorway with the plates.

AARATI
I got into a workshop. In Pokhara. Six weeks. I applied in
March and I didn't tell anyone because I knew.

Nobody says anything.

AARATI (CONT'D)
The seat goes on the tenth.

BABA
Today is the ninth.

AARATI
I know what today is.

She puts the tray down, properly, on the table. Goes out.

EXT. AARATI'S HOUSE - NIGHT

She sits on the step. The speaker starts up again inside.
Someone laughs.

Kanchha sits down next to her. Doesn't ask.

AARATI
He isn't wrong.

KANCHHA
He isn't right either.

AARATI
Both.

They sit.

KANCHHA
Ke garne ta?

AARATI
Next year they run it again.

KANCHHA
And next year?

AARATI
Next year I tell them in March.

INT. CALL CENTRE, NAXAL - NIGHT

Aarati at her desk. One headset. The clock: 11:02.

She takes the printed photograph of the Mustang road out of
her bag and tapes it to the partition wall.

She unmutes.

AARATI
Good evening, thank you for calling.

She writes something on a sticky note and presses it to the
monitor: MARCH.

FADE OUT.
